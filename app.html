    Settings

    Hold Ctrl+Alt to talk, or tap Ctrl+Alt+Space to lock it hands-free (press Ctrl+Alt to stop, Esc to cancel). The transcript is pasted into the focused app.

    Active provider

    Your provider choice is saved automatically and remembered after restarting.

    Live dictation

    Cloud dictations stream as you talk; the local model transcribes each phrase at your pauses, so only the last one is left when you stop. Parla then pastes the complete transcript. If this fails, Parla uses the saved recording.

    A discreet two-line preview. Your text box stays untouched until the final paste. With the local model, captions update phrase by phrase.

    Vocabulary

    One term per line, up to 100 terms of 50 characters. Optional recognition hints for supported cloud models. Italian and English are recognized automatically; hints do not rewrite your transcript.

    Hotkey

    With two or more modifiers (default Ctrl+Alt+Space), holding just the modifiers is push-to-talk and tapping the full shortcut locks it hands-free (press the modifiers again to stop). A shortcut like Ctrl+Shift+F9 is tap to start, tap to stop. Press “Record keys”, then hold the combination you want (Esc cancels). Rebinding takes effect immediately on save.

    Clipboard

    Use longer paste delays if an app misses or changes pasted text.

    How long Parla waits after pasting before restoring your previous clipboard (50–2000 ms). Raise it if a slow app pastes the old clipboard.
