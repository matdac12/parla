Parla Settings

Hold Ctrl+Alt to talk, or tap Ctrl+Alt+Space for hands-free. The transcript is pasted into the focused app.

Active provider

Hotkey

With two or more modifiers (default Ctrl+Alt+Space), holding just the modifiers is push-to-talk and tapping the full shortcut is hands-free (tap again to stop). A shortcut like Ctrl+Shift+F9 is tap to start, tap to stop. Press “Record keys”, then hold the combination you want (Esc cancels). Rebinding takes effect immediately on save.

Clipboard

How long Parla waits after pasting before restoring your previous clipboard (50–2000 ms). Raise it if a slow app pastes the old clipboard.

Settings are stored in the Parla app config folder. Close this window to keep Parla in the tray.
